⌚️🔒Benchmark for Non-reciprocal Attack Detection on Synchronization

Zishuo Ren, Yiting Lin, Yufei Zhang, Yang Li, Ziyang Chen, Hong Guo

2025, ArXiv:2512.12133

Using information theory, stochastic process and non-equilibrium statistical physics to quantify the security of high-precision time synchronization systems. We find that the stealthy attacks are not arbitrary, but follow the same overdamped Langevin dynamics as the environmental noise. Therefore we successfully quantify the security limit of a precision measurement system, and also quantitatively illustrate the intrinsic fiber noise (IFN) and delay unsuppressed noise (DUN).

Security is interesting and correlated with so many different regions. Maybe we'll do more later.🧐

📡📐The Shape of Information: Global Information Geometric Limits in Multi-task Quantum Systems

Zishuo Ren, Ziyang Chen, Hong Guo

2026, ArXiv:2607.28961

Using the information geometry and quantum information theory to establish a fundamental limit for quantum multi-task systems. We find that most of the quantum information tasks can be unified into a 3-step framwork. Therefore, despite their diverse metrics, we are able to find the universal information theoretic limit for the multi-task systems. The theorem indicates that one can not simply consider the amount of information, but its shape. Additionally, it takes the form of Shannon capacity theorem and somehow there are some traces of the epiperimetric problem.

We find it interesting that geometry and information theory can be so related.☝️🤓
