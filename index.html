About Me

Ray [Chinese Given Name: Zishuo(梓槊), Family Name: Ren(任)] received his B.S. in Space Physics from School of Earth and Space Science, Peking University, China, in June 2024. He is now working on his Ph.D. in School of Electronics, Peking University since September 2024.

His research interests include quantum information theory and technologies. Also interested in mythology(Chinese, Greek, Roman and North European), history (especially medival age, ancient middle east and Europe) and math. ^-^
